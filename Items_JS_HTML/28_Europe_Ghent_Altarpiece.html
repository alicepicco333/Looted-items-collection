dc:description Polyptych by Hubert and Jan van Eyck completed in 1432 for St Bavo's in Ghent, often called the most stolen artwork in history. Panels were taken to Paris by French revolutionary troops in 1794, sold and taken to Berlin, stolen in 1934, and seized by the Nazis before its recovery from the Altaussee salt mine in 1945.
