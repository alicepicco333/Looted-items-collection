dc:description Monumental gate of glazed bricks with dragons and bulls, built for Nebuchadnezzar II on the processional way of Babylon. Excavated by Robert Koldewey from 1902, shipped to Berlin and reconstructed in the Pergamon Museum from 1928. Iraqi officials have called for its return.
