dc:description Bronze astronomical instruments of the imperial observatory of Beijing, including armillary spheres, a celestial globe and a quadrant. Taken by French and German troops after the Boxer Rebellion in 1900, they were returned by France in 1902 and by Germany in 1921 under Article 131 of the Treaty of Versailles.
